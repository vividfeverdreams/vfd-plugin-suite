# YOI-BOI artwork

The working interface uses `HandheldShell.png`, derived from the original approved Play mockup with the built-in image-generation tool on 21 September 2026. The shell, empty screen, unlabelled physical button faces and circular knob caps are decorative artwork. All text, pointers, control states, the vowel graph, waveform, meters, and interactions are drawn and operated by native JUCE components. The waveform and meters use actual engine output.

The baked knob caps are rotationally symmetric; the native pointers and rings provide their live positions. The artwork is embedded in the VST3 and standalone binaries, so the installed plugin does not depend on external image files or a network connection.

Chakra Petch SemiBold and Bold Italic provide the labels and wordmark under the accompanying SIL Open Font License. YOI-BOI is original branding; no Nintendo or Game Boy logos are included.
