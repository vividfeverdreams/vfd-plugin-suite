# YOI-BOI 0.1.3 factory bank

These twelve sounds are hand-authored recipes for a clear opening vowel, a distinct transition and a bright reply. They are original patches, not renamed randomizer results or copies of commercial presets. **Tape Bounce** pursues a Tape B-inspired bounce direction; it is not an official artist preset, an endorsement or an exact reconstruction of a track.

Start around MIDI notes **36–48**. All patches have a clean **Direct** sub, **110 Hz body low-cut**, output at **−6 dB**, and a one-shot vowel gesture. MIDI note duration controls how long the resulting sound sustains. The bank deliberately keeps Dirt moderate; increase it after settling on the vowel movement.

| Preset | Family | Vowel motion | Synced length | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tape Bounce** | Rubber | OO → AH → EE | 1/8 | Rounded attack opens into a crisp reply. The default for a bouncing call-and-response bass phrase. |
| **Bubble Trouble** | Rubber | OO → OH → EE | 1/8 triplet | A brief rounded hold before the bright turn, with lower harmonic density for rolling triplet phrases. |
| **Elastic Yoi** | Rubber | OH → AH → EE | 1/8 dotted | A slower, wider mouth opening with a longer vowel tail between rhythmic stabs. |
| **Chrome Chew** | Metal | OO → EH → EE | 1/8 | A bright harmonic edge with a quick consonant-like opening and clear upper vowel. |
| **Laser Reply** | Metal | OH → EH → EE | 1/16 | Short, high-register articulation for quick answers and fills. |
| **Steel Syllable** | Metal | AH → OH → EE | 1/8 triplet | A deliberate dip into a darker middle vowel before the crisp finish; the densest factory character. |
| **Low Talker** | Throat | OO → AH → EE | 1/4 | A deep, broad opening with enough time to hear each stage of the mouth movement. |
| **Vowel Throw** | Throat | OH → AH → EE | 1/8 dotted | Pronounced middle-vowel projection and a longer bright throw at the end. |
| **Basement Answer** | Throat | OO → OH → EH | 1/4 triplet | A darker, less piercing reply with restrained drive and a rounded sub foundation. |
| **Pocket Gremlin** | Squeak | OH → EH → EE | 1/16 | Compact, bright stabs with a fast transition for call-and-response punctuation. |
| **Squeaky Clean** | Squeak | OO → AH → EE | 1/16 triplet | The least driven patch: a short, open-to-bright chirp for tight triplet accents. |
| **Neon Chirp** | Squeak | AH → EH → EE | 1/8 | An open attack followed by a focused, higher vowel for melodic bass replies. |

For a separate sub track, select **Sub Off** and keep **Low Cut** on. For more grit inside YOI-BOI, try **Driven** and raise Dirt gradually. Sub Off removes the dedicated oscillator; Low Cut reduces the body signal's lower frequencies.

The **Length feel selector** changes straight, triplet and dotted timing without changing the original division parameter's identity. The release demos use the actual plug-in processor at **140 BPM** with the same notes and velocities across the bank to make comparison easier.

Factory IDs remain `factory:0` through `factory:11`. Their revised recipes may change the result of selecting a factory preset compared with 0.1.2. Existing saved user patches and the current sound in an older Live Set retain their stored parameter values, while the improved audio engine can still change their timbre.
