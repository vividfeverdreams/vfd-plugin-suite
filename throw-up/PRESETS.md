# THROW-UP factory presets

50 sounds for mid-bass reverb throws: ten each in **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Huge**, and **Creative**.

Automate **THROW** in your DAW to choose when the reverb is heard. It controls a parallel wet return: 0% gives the original signal, and 100% gives the original signal plus the full reverb return. The dry gain stays at unity before Output trim. A short 4 ms ramp smooths full-range moves. Factory preset loading preserves THROW, output, and bypass so changing the room does not overwrite your performance automation.

Every preset stores a neutral 0% THROW and 0 dB output. The bank does not contain beat-synced or self-triggering throws. Predelay is in milliseconds; decay is the nominal feedback RT60 in seconds. Filtering and modulation affect the audible frequency-dependent decay. Wet low-cut filtering protects the low end, while dry bass remains unchanged at 0% THROW and unity output.

Room size sets reflection spacing; decay controls persistence. The Creative group deliberately combines unusual sizes, long or short decays, strong motion, band limiting, and saturation. Grit saturates only the wet return; duck reduces that return while input is present. These presets do not use pitch shifting or reverse processing.
