VIVID FEVER DREAMS SUITE 1.6.1 - BETA INSTALL

Includes YOI-BOI 0.2.3, THROW-UP 0.2.2, BACK-SCRATCH 0.11.2,
SHIFTY 0.5.3, ENERGY HARVESTER 0.4.3, and STUTTER-STEP 0.4.1.

NEW IN 1.6.1
Random presets: every plug-in has a die or shuffle key next to its
preset name that loads a random saved preset, never the one already
loaded.
SHIFTY: RANDOM now switches the engine and Vowel Color too.
Right-click the engine selector to lock it.
Linux: the suite now also runs on 64-bit Linux (VST3), as a separate
download. Thanks to Rolandjg for building the Linux version.
Already on 1.6.0? Open any plug-in's INFO page and click INSTALL.
Every plug-in comes as a VST3 and as an Audio Unit (AU) on the Mac,
signed with the developer's Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
The suite also runs on Windows and Linux (VST3), as separate downloads.

REQUIREMENTS
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later.
VST3: Ableton Live, Bitwig, FL Studio, REAPER and other VST3 hosts.
AU: Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage and other AU hosts.

INSTALL
1. Double-click the ZIP to extract it.
2. Quit your audio applications (including Logic Pro or GarageBand if
   you are installing the Audio Units).
3. Double-click Install Vivid Fever Dreams Suite.app. If macOS asks
   whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, click Open.
4. Tick the plug-ins and formats you want and click Install / Update.
5. Follow the rescan steps the installer shows for your DAW.

INSTALL LOCATIONS AND SUPPORT
VST3: ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
AU:   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
No administrator password is needed. The installer reports conflicts,
such as a second copy in the computer-wide plug-in folders or a newer
version, instead of overwriting them. Uninstall moves plug-ins to the
Trash, so you can put them back.
Installation details: README.md
Log: ~/Library/Logs/Vivid Fever Dreams Suite/installer.log
